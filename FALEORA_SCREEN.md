# FALEORA Screen

Partner mekanlardaki TV, LED, tablet veya projeksiyonlari interaktif deneyim ekranina donusturen urun katmani.

## Akis
FALEORA check-in -> ayri screen opt-in -> Welcome -> Passport/Reward -> opsiyonel UGC.

## Moduller
Welcome, Social Wall, QR Photo Booth, Live Poll, Reactions, Lucky Table, Live Challenges, Birthday Moment, Couple Moment, FALEORA Night, Screen Games, Passport Pulse, Campaign/Ads, Moderation ve Analytics.

## Privacy
Check-in kamusal ekranda gorunme izni degildir. Foto+isim, avatar+isim, sadece isim veya gorunmeme secenekleri ayri consent olarak tutulur.

## Production
Realtime WebSocket/SSE, media storage, moderation, signed device pairing, rate limits, consent records, analytics pipeline ve offline screen cache gerekir.
